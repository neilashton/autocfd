About AutoCFD5

AutoCFD5 was held on 8–9 October 2026 at DAMA Technopole, Bologna, Italy, with Upstream CFD as the main event organiser, CINECA providing the venue and local organisational support, and EVCM as financial administrator. It followed AutoCFD1 in Oxford (2019), AutoCFD2 in Berlin (2021), AutoCFD3 in Barcelona (2022) and AutoCFD4 in Belfast (2024).

The workshop brought together automotive CFD researchers and practitioners from academia and industry to assess predictive capability for road-car geometries and discuss practical modelling guidance. Topics included turbulence modelling, meshing, numerical schemes, high-performance computing, noise factors and AI/ML.

The Windsor Squareback and DrivAer Notchback benchmark resources remain available for reference and further study. For the AI/ML activity, the open-source DrivAerML dataset was the standard training dataset used to assess model architectures.
